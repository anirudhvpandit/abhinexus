# Portfolio case study: Northstar Holdings

**Synthetic demonstration only.** Northstar Holdings, its people, projects, dates, amounts, risk statements, and allocation figures are generated sample data. This case study is not a customer story, validated outcome, or investment claim.

## Context

The synthetic portfolio contains five transformation and operating projects across Digital Transformation, Operations, and Risk & Compliance. The intended demonstration problem is fragmented executive visibility across delivery status, forecast cost, capacity, and risk.

## Portfolio position

- Approved budget: **$9.79m**
- Actual recorded cost: **$5.455m**
- Forecast cost: **$9.92m**
- Forecast variance (budget − forecast): **−$130k**
- Projects flagged at risk: **2 of 5**
- Completed projects: **0**, so on-time delivery is not measurable.

Two at-risk projects, Field Ops Intelligence and Cyber Defense Program, forecast $460k above their individual budgets. Other projects contribute $330k of forecast headroom; this does not authorize transfers between project budgets.

## Key risks and decisions

1. **Cyber Defense Program — Leah Frost:** validate remediation scope, remaining cost, and control-validation milestones; decide whether the $290k overrun needs a formal forecast/change-control review.
2. **Field Ops Intelligence — Deepak Singh:** validate adoption assumptions and response plan; review the $170k forecast overrun before the next steering review.
3. **ERP Modernization — Priya Nair:** confirm forecast headroom and supplier-response mitigation before relying on $190k under-budget forecast.
4. **Portfolio owner — Alicia Chen (synthetic sponsor):** reconcile aggregate forecasts and clarify decision rights before reallocating headroom.

Names and actions are proposed for demo storytelling; no real owners have accepted these actions.
