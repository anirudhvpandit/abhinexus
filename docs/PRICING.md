# Pricing hypotheses

**Status: research hypothesis only.** No customer interviews, willingness-to-pay study, competitive pricing validation, payment integration, or revenue results are available. Do not quote the following as approved commercial prices.

## Positioning hypothesis

AbhiNexus is intended for organizations that need portfolio-level delivery, finance, risk, and resource visibility beyond a collection of team task boards. Likely buyers are PMO leaders, COO/CFO organizations, transformation offices, and mid-market/enterprise project portfolios. These profiles must be validated.

## Packaging hypothesis

| Candidate package | Potential buyer/value | Candidate boundary to validate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Small PMO proving a unified project register | Limited active portfolios/projects, core reports, standard support. |
| Professional | Multi-team PMO needing finance, risk, and capacity visibility | More active projects, role-based finance controls, portfolio exports. |
| Business | Larger, cross-functional portfolio with administrative needs | Multiple portfolios, expanded governance, audit and onboarding support. |
| Enterprise | Regulated or complex organization | Contract-based pricing, deployment/security review, service commitments, and separately scoped SSO/integrations only when actually available. |

The plan names exist in subscription metadata, but package entitlements are not enforced. The current plan simulator is not billing.

## Commercial model to test

Compare (1) annual organization/portfolio contract pricing, (2) active-project bands, and (3) seat-based pricing. The brief recommends contract-based pricing; test procurement fit and value metric with target buyers before selecting a model. Avoid charging per contributor if it discourages adoption; validate infrastructure/support costs before offering unlimited use.

## Research before setting numbers

Interview PMO and finance buyers across organization size bands; ask about current tooling, project counts, audit requirements, budget ownership, implementation effort, procurement ceilings, and alternatives. Test packaging with realistic concept cards and a paid pilot proposal. Record sample size and responses; do not convert informal interest into revenue claims.

## Commercial prerequisites

Payment provider, tax/invoice flow, subscription entitlements, cancellation/refund policy, terms/privacy, support SLAs, data processing terms, onboarding, sales qualification, and support capacity are not implemented. See [COMMERCIAL_LAUNCH_PLAN.md](./COMMERCIAL_LAUNCH_PLAN.md).
